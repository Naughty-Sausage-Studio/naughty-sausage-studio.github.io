Privacy Policy

Splinter: Garage to the Stars, by adrianworks. Last updated: 21 October 2026 (version 1.1.0).

This policy explains what information Splinter: Garage to the Stars ("the game") uses, and why. In short: the game itself keeps your progress only on your device. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure those ads.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, parts, best distance, missions, sound settings and when you last saw an ad) in a file on your own device. adrianworks does not receive this file. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, and an occasional full-screen ad between flights. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This information goes to Google, not to adrianworks. It is sent over an encrypted connection. Google uses it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you play, you are asked whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • Playing offline: the game works without an internet connection. No ads are shown and no information is sent while you are offline.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in, and asks for no name, email address or other contact details.
  • It does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos or other files.
  • adrianworks does not sell your information, and does not run its own analytics or tracking.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and want information about them removed from Google's advertising systems, contact us and we will help you with the request.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is used (for example by adding in-app purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
